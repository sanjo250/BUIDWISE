# 🏢 BuildWise

### Sustainable Building Energy Assessment System

BuildWise is a web-based sustainability assessment platform that helps users analyze the energy performance of buildings and explore strategies for reducing energy consumption, operational costs, and environmental impact.

The system enables users to create a building profile, evaluate electricity consumption patterns, calculate Energy Performance Indicators (EPI), and simulate sustainability improvements through interactive what-if scenarios.

---

## 🌍 Problem Statement

Buildings are among the largest consumers of energy worldwide and contribute significantly to carbon emissions. However, many building owners and occupants lack simple tools to understand how their energy usage affects sustainability and operating costs.

BuildWise addresses this challenge by providing an easy-to-use digital platform that helps users:

- Understand their building's energy performance
- Identify opportunities for energy savings
- Estimate environmental impact
- Explore sustainable improvement strategies
- Make informed decisions for greener buildings

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 🏢 Building Profile Creation
Create and manage building profiles using essential building information.

### ⚡ Energy Performance Assessment
Analyze annual electricity consumption and evaluate building efficiency.

### 📊 Energy Performance Indicator (EPI)
Calculate and display EPI values for performance benchmarking.

### 📈 Monthly Energy Visualization
Visualize monthly electricity consumption through interactive charts.

### 🔄 What-If Simulation
Test sustainability measures and estimate their impact on:

- Energy consumption
- Operating costs
- Carbon emissions

### 🌱 Sustainability Insights
Receive recommendations to improve energy efficiency and environmental performance.

### 📄 Report Export
Generate and export assessment summaries for documentation and analysis.
