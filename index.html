05 / METHOD & LIMITATIONS

Simple, transparent calculations.

BUILDWISE is a student-level decision-support prototype. It is not a calibrated digital twin, professional audit, or substitute for detailed engineering simulation.

01

Energy Performance Index

EPI = Annual kWh ÷ Floor area (m²)
02

Energy saved

Baseline kWh × Reduction (%)
03

Bill saving

Energy saved × Tariff (₹/kWh)
04

Avoided emissions

Energy saved × Grid factor (kg CO₂/kWh)

Important: Emission factors vary by location and reporting year. Replace the default with an appropriate documented factor. Simple payback is cost divided by estimated annual bill savings; it excludes maintenance, financing, incentives and changes in energy prices.
